🚀 Rocket Impact

Multiplayer asteroid survival. Fly your rocket, blast asteroids and try to stay alive.

How to play
• Move your finger or mouse to control your rocket.
• Tap/click an asteroid to destroy it.
• Explosions deflect nearby asteroids.
• Bigger asteroids can split into smaller ones.
• If an asteroid hits your rocket, you lose a life.
Multiplayer connection will be enabled when the server is configured.
